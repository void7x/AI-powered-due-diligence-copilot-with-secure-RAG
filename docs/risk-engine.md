# Risk & opportunity engines

## Design principle

Detection is **deterministic**; the LLM, when configured, is used only for narrative enrichment. Findings are derived from financial facts and extracted signals rather than model-generated scores.

A risk finding includes:

```text
category, title, severity(low|medium|high|critical), score(0-100),
signals, explanation, why_it_matters, potential_impact, confidence,
evidence(document, page, quote), recommendation
```

## Risk rule families

The engine covers leverage, liquidity, cash conversion, margin pressure, customer concentration, supplier concentration, interest-rate exposure, FX exposure, working capital, capex intensity, covenant/refinancing language, execution/expansion risk, regulatory/compliance exposure, cybersecurity, governance/key-person signals, competitive pressure and disclosure consistency.

Severity is derived from deterministic thresholds and score magnitudes; there is no randomness and no LLM scoring.

Each persisted finding can carry `risk_evidence` rows containing the source document, page and quote. Those rows power provenance shown in the UI and report.

## Scorecards

The company overview exposes five 0–100 scorecards: Overall Risk, Financial Health, Growth Potential, Operational Risk and Governance Risk.

Overall-risk labels use these bands:

```text
low       < 25
moderate  25–49
 elevated 50–74
high      >= 75
```

The component scores use deterministic financial/risk calculations and are never generated by the LLM.

## Opportunity engine

Opportunity detection is also rule-based and covers revenue momentum, margin expansion, backlog/demand signals, R&D pipeline, international expansion, capacity investment, pricing power, balance-sheet capacity and efficiency/product-mix signals. Findings carry confidence and evidence provenance where a source can be resolved.

## Cross-document inconsistency detection

Narrative claims can be compared with extracted reported values or other document claims. Potential mismatches are recorded neutrally, with both sides and their source pages where available. The intended wording is:

> "Potential inconsistency detected. Further investigation recommended."

## Management questions

Questions are generated from detected risks and opportunities. Each persisted question includes a topic, rationale, priority and a link to the underlying analysis context so analysts can turn findings into a focused management agenda.
